Options

Difficulty guide

Easy

Three values only; small + and − puzzles.

Normal

Compact puzzles; adds × and frequent three-value rounds.

Hard

Compact puzzles; adds integer ÷ and some three-value rounds.

Expert

Adds modulus % and powers ^, with occasional three-value rounds.

Extreme

Largest values and every operation, including √, in the longest puzzles.

Every mode follows a repeating eight-round wave: warm-ups, standard rounds, and challenge rounds average back to the mode’s normal difficulty. The wave averages exactly over typical 24, 32, and 40-puzzle sessions.

Accessibility & sound

Keyboard: Left/Right selects a number, Space flips it, Enter checks, and H gives a hint.
