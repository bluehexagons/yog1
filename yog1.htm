Difficulty guide

Easy

Three values with + and −.

Normal

Short puzzles with × and frequent three-value rounds.

Hard

Short puzzles with integer ÷ and some three-value rounds.

Expert

Adds % and ^, with some three-value rounds.

Extreme

Longer puzzles with every operation, including √.

Each mode repeats an eight-round wave of warm-ups, standard rounds, and gentler challenges.
